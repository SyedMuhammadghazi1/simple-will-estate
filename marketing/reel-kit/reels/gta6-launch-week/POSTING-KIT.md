# Posting kit: "GTA 6 needs up to 212 gigs. Got room?"

An unofficial, fan-made launch-week explainer. Every line was checked on 15 November 2026
against Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony and Microsoft sources (table at the end). Some Rockstar pages
would not load for the automated checks, so their wording was read from reprints: step 2 below
asks you to look at three of them yourself.

## Before you post

1. **It has a shelf life.** The main cut says "drops this Thursday", "pre-load is already open"
   and "start it tonight". Post it between 15 and 17 November: the game unlocks in New Zealand
   on the morning of the 18th (US time), and a reel keeps being shown for days after you post
   it. From Thursday 19 November post the `out-now` cut, and archive the main cut or add a line
   to its caption saying it was made before launch.
2. **Open three pages yourself before you post** and confirm the wording the reel attributes to
   Rockstar:
   - the [Support article on release times](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s):
     "midnight local time on November 19";
   - the [Newswire post of 3 November](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10):
     "up to 212 GB depending on your device";
   - the [Newswire post of 10 November](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now):
     "targeting 30 frames per second".
3. **Re-check two lines on the day you post**, because they can change without warning:
   - the date: <https://www.rockstargames.com/VI> should still say "Coming November 19, 2026";
   - "PC players? Nothing announced.": check the [Rockstar Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire).
     If a PC version has been announced, do not post either cut.
4. **Keep it fan-made.** The reel uses no Rockstar logo, footage, key art or music: only the
   game's name in plain text and original drawings, with "Fan-made · not affiliated with
   Rockstar" on screen throughout and a full line on the end card. Do not add game footage,
   screenshots, the logo or the soundtrack in Instagram's editor. Rockstar's published policy
   tolerates non-commercial fan content and takes down leaks and spoilers; a reel that carries
   none of their material does not depend on that policy, but adding their material would.
5. **Post it from a personal or fan account, and do not boost it.** Rockstar's policy defines
   non-commercial narrowly (not earning from it, not part of a promotion for a product or
   service) and does not cover advertisements. In particular, do not post it on the Plainwill
   account or link it to any product: the reel shares its layout and its mascot's shape with
   the Plainwill reel (the mascot is re-coloured here), and on a brand's account it would read
   as that brand's promotion. If it has to go on a business account, ask a lawyer first. Do
   not use the paid partnership label: nobody paid for this.
6. **The voice is synthetic.** Turn on Instagram's AI label when you publish; Meta requires it
   for realistic-sounding AI audio.

None of this is legal advice.

## Caption

```
GTA 6 launches Thursday, November 19. Here's what to sort out first.

1. Space: Rockstar says the pre-load needs up to 212 GB, depending on your device. Going by the usable space reviewers measured, that is about a third of an original PS5 and more than half of a 512 GB Xbox Series S.
2. Pre-load opened November 10 for digital pre-orders. Boxed copies are a download code, no disc, so everyone is downloading.
3. Unlock: Rockstar says midnight local time on November 19. Check the countdown on your own console.
4. Frame rate: Rockstar says it is "targeting 30 frames per second".
5. Price in the US: $79.99, or $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition.
6. PC: nothing has been announced.

How much did you have to delete to make room?

Unofficial fan explainer. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Sources: Rockstar Games Newswire and Support, rockstargames.com/VI, Rockstar Store, PlayStation Blog (November 10, 2026), Take-Two press release (June 24, 2026), launch reviews of the PS5 and Xbox Series S (2020) for usable space. Checked November 15, 2026.

#gta6 #gtavi #gaming #ps5 #xboxseriess
```

  is the signal Instagram has said weighs most for reaching people who don't follow you.
