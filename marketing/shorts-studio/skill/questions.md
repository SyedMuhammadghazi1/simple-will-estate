# The questions

Send the block between the two `---` lines as ONE message, word for word. Skip anything they already told you.
"go" means yes to every default.

---

Before I start, 7 quick questions (defaults in brackets, say "go" to take them all):

1. **Your files.** Where is the recording? If your camera and your microphone recorded separately (for example a camera file plus a screen recording that has the good mic), give me both. [one file]
2. **Long or short?** Is this already one Short, or a long video I should find Shorts inside? [one Short]
3. **Your look.** Pick a preset (clean, night, bold, paper), or give me your brand colours, font and 1 to 3 examples you like. [night]
4. **Layout.** Split (your screen or proof on top, you below) or full (just you, cropped tall)? [split if there is a screen recording, else full]
5. **Graphics.** How busy? Light (title, 2 cards, end card) or more (a card about every 8 s). Anything I must show (a number, a product, a link)? [light]
6. **Ending.** Ask people to comment a word? Which word? [yes, I'll pick one from the topic]
7. **Epidemic Sound.** Do you want to use Epidemic Sound for sound effects and music? If yes, I'll open it in my own browser (Claude's built-in browser), you sign in once with your normal account, and I find and download the tracks for you. [no: I use the free sound kit, and music only if you give me a file]

Optional: an Auphonic key in a `.env` file (clean studio voice) and a music file you own the rights to (or say yes to question 7).

## If they are unsure
- **Style:** pick the preset that fits the topic (tech or screen-heavy = night, calm advice = clean, hooks and lists = bold, story or notes = paper) and say why in one line.
- **Animation:** keep the defaults. Cards slide in on their word, numbers count up, captions pop word by word. Do not add more movement than that unless they ask.
- **Ending word:** one short, concrete word from the topic, in capitals ("UPDATE", "PROMPT", "GUIDE").
