# Principles: editing a talking-head recording into a 9:16 Short

The rules behind the cut, not one creator's look. Colours, ground, fonts and mascot all come from **the user's style**.
Built 26 Oct 2026 from CRITERIA.md, ledger/RULES.md (R01 to R44), rules/RULES.md + RESEARCH.md, the reel hook lab and the 26 Oct notes.
Every check that enforces these is in `CHECKS.md`.

Sources in brackets:
- [hook lab n=55] = 55 viral Reels from 42 creators, 5.8x to 288x their own median.
- [measured: 25 videos] = 10 Nick Saraev + 10 creator long-form + the creator's top 5 Shorts, measured from frames. Shorts numbers come from the 5 Shorts.
- [creator note R##] = a verdict the creator gave, saved as a rule. [creator note 26 Oct] = the newest notes.
- [approved Short] = the round the creator signed off (r11). [endings study n=57] = 57 viral Reels' endings.
- Newest note wins when two numbers disagree.

Layouts: **split** (graphic on top, speaker below), **full graphic**, **speaker full screen**, **b-roll** (real footage).

## Hook (first 5 s)

1. Talk from frame 0. First word by 0.5 s (87% of viral Reels). [hook lab n=55]
2. Say about 10 words in the first 3 s (8.5 to 14). [hook lab n=55]
3. Something moves by 0.3 s. First animation at a median 0.2 s, always by 0.7 s. [hook lab n=55]
4. Words on screen by 1.0 s (91% show text in the first second). [hook lab n=55]
5. Frame 0 shows one legible thing: a big title, the real logos or the real UI. 36 of 55 show a headline. [hook lab n=55]
6. Copy one proven hook recipe. Split with proof on top is the most common (14 Reels, 26x median). [hook lab n=55]
7. Proof first plus "I built..." scores highest (4 Reels, 66x median). [hook lab n=55]
8. Keep the motion inside the frame early. The first hard cut lands about 4.4 s in (2.4 to 9.1). [hook lab n=55]
9. First 3 s: 4 to 6 visible changes, no gap over 1.0 s. [measured: 25 videos]
10. Proof beats polish: the top half of breakouts cut less and used fewer SFX in 0 to 3 s. [hook lab n=55]
11. The first second is cut as hard as the rest: no warm-up, no "so to... so to". [creator note R32]

## Structure & timing

12. Length 27 to 62 s. Most viral Reels land near 39 s. [hook lab n=55]
13. Plan in 3 s rows: one message, one layout, one hero visual, one real asset each. A layout holds a median 3.0 s. [measured: 25 videos]
14. One idea per shot. Each shot 2 to 5 s. [creator note 26 Oct]
15. No shot under 1.5 s. 12 of 26 shots under 1.2 s read "too frantic"; the approved Short had 1 of 18, median 2.1 s. [creator note 26 Oct]
16. Something visibly changes about every 0.8 to 1.1 s (9 to 12.7 changes per 10 s). [measured: 25 videos]
17. A held hero with motion inside it (typing, waves, a cursor) counts as change. Not every change is a cut. [creator note R20]
18. The longest still moment is 3 s or less. [measured: 25 videos]
19. A layout switch or hard cut every 1.5 to 2.7 s: 8 to 12 switches in 40 s. [measured: 25 videos]
20. Viral Reels are calmer in the body: a visual change every 2.6 s, a hard cut every 5.6 s. [hook lab n=55]
21. Cut on meaning: each switch lands on a phrase boundary or on the word the next graphic is about. [creator note R43]
22. The sentence picks the layout: a product named gets its logo or real UI full screen (5 of 5). [measured: 25 videos]
23. A number gets a count-up full screen (6 of 7). A list builds as a checklist (4 of 6). [measured: 25 videos]
24. "How it works" gets a small diagram. Proof goes in a split (5 runs). [measured: 25 videos]
25. Opinions, turns and punchlines go on the speaker's face (9 of 11). [measured: 25 videos]

## Layout & framing

26. Rotate four layouts: split, full graphic, speaker full, b-roll. Never one talking head with cards on top. [creator note R30]
27. Layout shares: split 31 to 40%, graphics about 49%, speaker full 11 to 20%. [measured: 25 videos]
28. The speaker is on screen 45 to 56% of the runtime (viral Reels: 64%). [measured: 25 videos] [hook lab n=55]
29. Never more than 2.5 s without the speaker's face. Cover a bad moment with a SHORT graphic, then come back. [creator note R38]
30. Split = 50/50, seam at 50%, no divider, no gap, both panels edge to edge. [measured: 25 videos]
31. In a split the face is 40 to 44% of the panel height (aim 41%), eyes 32 to 39% down. Zoom out, never in. [measured: 25 videos]
32. Show head, shoulders and mic in the split. [measured: 25 videos]
33. Speaker full screen: face 38 to 42% of frame height, eyes 33 to 42% down. [measured: 25 videos]
34. Speaker full holds 6 s at most; a split holds 7.5 s at most. [measured: 25 videos]
35. 9:16 is full-bleed: no bands, no empty panels. [creator note R33]
36. Never put graphics over the face. Measure the real tracked face box, never a guessed one. [creator note R18]
37. Keep graphics out of the caption band and the platform's top and bottom UI. [build spec]
38. Layout changes animate: the graphic grows from the top panel to full, the speaker slides. Never a hard snap mid-object. [creator note 26 Oct]

## Cutting

39. Cut every stutter, repeat and false start. [creator note R32]
40. Find repeats by listening, not only by reading. Whisper merged "and fixes and fixes" into one copy. [creator note 26 Oct]
41. Drop the first copy of a repeat and keep the clean take. [creator note R37]
42. No pause over 0.2 s anywhere, including under a full graphic. [creator note 26 Oct]
43. Never a naked jump cut. Hide every jump under a layout switch, a second angle, footage or a graphic. [creator note R15]
44. Check the raw clip for its own jump cuts before planning layouts. [creator note R15]
45. Never show the speaker looking down (script, desk, floor). Cut tight or cover it. [creator note 26 Oct]
46. Eyes on the lens. Turned 12 deg or more for 0.8 s is a fail; second angles stay front-facing. [creator note R16]
47. No silent stare: never 0.2 s of the speaker on screen not talking. [creator note R17]
48. No camera moves on the speaker: no punch-ins, pushes, drifts or fast zoom in and out. [creator note R13, R14]
49. Lip sync is frame-exact: 0 frames off (1 allowed). Rebuild the camera files after every re-cut. [creator note 26 Oct]
50. No rough cut at the start or the end. [creator note R32]

## Graphics & motion

51. Show, don't echo: never type the word just said, never a label chip. Big centred kinetic text is the one exception. [creator note R03]
52. Show, don't tell: type a real prompt and show its real result. No abstract labels or icon tiles. [creator note R27]
53. Real assets only: real logos, real UI, real footage. Never grey stand-ins. [creator note R02]
54. Real UI moves: push in, click, type, scroll, the result pops. Never a still screenshot. [creator note R31]
55. Real product footage full screen is the strongest beat. [creator note R24]
56. One screen, one hero. The hero is 65%+ wide full screen (median 86%), 58%+ in a split top. [measured: 25 videos]
57. A full graphic fills the frame: hero 80%+ wide, real UI or one strong object. [creator note R41]
58. Worlds, not cards: 5 to 8 objects that each live 2.5 to 10 s and carry across cuts. [creator note 26 Oct]
59. Never a new unrelated card on every cut. It reads as "spasming". [creator note 26 Oct]
60. At most 2 new elements within any 0.5 s. Never a cluster of small pops. [creator note 26 Oct]
61. Calm: land, settle, hold. No idle wobble, swing, hop or drift. [creator note 26 Oct]
62. Camera moves inside a graphic: about 1 per second at most, each 0.6 s or longer, soft eases, hold 0.3 to 0.8 s. [creator note 26 Oct]
63. Enter with a pop or a build, 0.1 to 0.3 s per element. A count-up takes about 0.6 s. [measured: 25 videos]
64. No slide, wipe or typewriter on the main element. [measured: 25 videos]
65. Each graphic means something for the line and lands on its word within 2 frames (fail at 3). [creator note R19]
66. Every visual does something while the speaker talks. [creator note R06]
67. Arrows and cursors touch what they are about. Overlays never cover the thing shown. [creator note R05, R34]
68. Proof stays calm: one clean visual, not chips, stamps or a busy count-up. [creator note R07]
69. Progress is a picture (a track filling), never a text counter. [creator note R08]
70. No AI cliches: sparkle stars, crossed-out icons, restart arrows, glowing orbs. [creator note R04]
71. Logos come from the brand's own source at full size, clean, never a cropped favicon. [creator note R40]
72. Space on an 8 px grid: 32 px between blocks, 48 px margins, 28 px padding, nothing touching. [creator note R10]
73. Centre all text (within 7%). [creator note R12]
74. Look: one ground and one accent per scene, at most 2 fonts, all from the user's style. [research: 20 outlier Reels, W6]

## Captions

75. The caption carries the sentence. The graphic carries the noun or the number. [measured: 25 videos]
76. 1 to 3 words per caption, mostly 2. [measured: 25 videos]
77. One caption at a time, never two lines stacked. [creator note 25 Oct]
78. Text 54 to 70 px (2.8 to 3.6% of height) on a see-through pill about 75 px tall, styled from the user's style. [measured: 25 videos]
79. On the seam in a split. 66 to 68% down in other layouts. Never on the face. [measured: 25 videos]
80. Captions live on their own layer, so they switch off without moving anything else. [measured: 25 videos]
81. At most 3 words + 1 number per graphic and 8 words per screen (10 on a real clip). [creator note R11]
82. Fix misheard names before captioning. Check the grammar of every line on screen. [creator note 25 Oct]

## Sound

83. Every sound effect names the visible action that causes it: click, type, land, swipe. [creator note 26 Oct]
84. Never put a sound on an abstract word. [creator note 26 Oct]
85. Level each cue by its event loudness: median 10 to 14 dB under the voice, big hits 6 to 9, keys 16 to 20. [approved Short]
86. The whole SFX stem sits 12 to 17 dB under the voice. [approved Short]
87. About 6 effects per 10 s, at most 8 in any 10 s. [approved Short]
88. Never two effects within 0.2 s. Never the same file twice in a row. [creator note R39] [approved Short]
89. Music bed from 0 s, 13 to 14 dB under the voice, ducked under big hits, 0.8 s fade out. [creator note R35]
90. 44% of viral Reels use a bed, 12 dB under the voice. 56% use none. [hook lab n=55]
91. Master at -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP. [hook lab n=55: viral mix -14.2 LUFS]
92. Licensed music and effects only. No home-made synth whooshes. [creator note R35, R39]
93. Numbers are not enough. Listen to the final before it ships. [creator note R39]

## Ending

94. End on an ask: "Comment WORD and I'll send you X" (37 of 55). [hook lab n=55]
95. The CTA grows out of the last graphic. No collage, no flashy "comment" animation. [creator note r9]
96. Default ending: the speaker full screen with the keyword big at chest height (21 of 37 comment-CTA Reels). [endings study n=57]
97. That default is not proven to convert better. The samples disagree (p = 0.10 to 0.22). [endings study n=57]
98. An end card, if used, holds 4.0 to 4.5 s. Never signal the end, no goodbye. [measured: 25 videos]
99. No rough cut on the last frame. [creator note R32]

## House rules

- A note given once applies to every future edit. A note that repeats becomes a rule, a judge question and a test.
- Ship only when the code gate passes and the eye test has 0 NO. Borderline is NO.
- Look at the frames, not just the numbers.
